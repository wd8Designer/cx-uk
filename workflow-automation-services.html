The Real Problem

When Coordination Between Systems Falls on People

Most workflows do not fail because a single task is hard; they fail because moving work between people and systems relies on someone remembering to follow up. Approvals sit in inboxes, status updates get missed, and handoffs between teams depend on manual chasing. Workflow automation removes that coordination burden by triggering the next step automatically once conditions are met.

Manual follow-up stalls approvals and handoffs.

Status updates get missed without a system tracking progress.
