The Real Problem

When a Virtual Assistant Needs to Do More Than Talk

A chat interface that only answers questions solves half the problem. Customers and employees usually want something done, an order checked, a meeting booked, a request submitted, not just explained. Without access to the right systems and a clear boundary around what it can act on, a virtual assistant becomes another place people ask questions and still have to complete the task manually somewhere else.

Assistants without system access can inform but cannot resolve requests.

Undefined permissions create risk once an assistant can take action.

No escalation path leaves complex requests stuck mid conversation.

Disconnected tools mean duplicate work across chat and existing systems.

Task Completion

Controlled Access

Human Escalation

Requests outside its scope move to a person without losing context.

Built to Scale

The same assistant supports one team or an entire organisation.

Industry Use

Virtual Assistants Built Around Your Industry

The right virtual assistant depends on the tasks it needs to complete, the systems it must reach and how much autonomy is appropriate for each request.

Retail and Ecommerce

Retail assistants handle order tracking, returns and account updates directly against live systems, resolving requests without a support ticket.

  • Updates order and delivery status without manual lookup.
  • Processes straightforward returns within defined rules.
  • Escalates payment disputes to staff automatically.

Financial Services

Financial services assistants support account queries and routine service requests, while keeping transactions and sensitive changes behind authentication and review.

  • Restricts financial actions to verified, authenticated sessions.
  • Handles routine account queries without staff involvement.
  • Routes complex or regulated requests to the right team.

Professional Services

Professional services assistants manage scheduling, document requests and status updates, freeing staff from repetitive coordination work.

  • Books and reschedules appointments directly against calendars.
  • Retrieves document or case status without manual checking.
  • Flags requests needing judgement to the responsible person.

Enterprise Operations

Internal assistants give employees direct access to HR, IT and operational requests without routing everything through a helpdesk.

  • Submits and tracks internal requests within existing systems.
  • Answers policy questions from approved internal documentation.
  • Escalates unresolved issues to the relevant department.
