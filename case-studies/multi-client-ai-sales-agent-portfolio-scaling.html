UK Digital Agency Case Study: Scaling an AI Sales Agent Across a Multi-Client eCommerce Portfolio

A UK digital marketing agency teamed up with Cypherox to deploy an AI sales agent that operates across multiple client storefronts simultaneously. This gave the agency a conversion tool they could offer across their entire UK client base, rather than building a separate solution for each account.

Where We Started

Overview

The agency manages eCommerce results for several UK client brands and needed a way to improve conversions across all of them without rebuilding each storefront individually. A single, configurable system was the only way to make this work at portfolio scale.

Problem Statement

Visitors were arriving at client stores but not purchasing at the rates clients required. Addressing this store by store was not something the agency's team could take on without significant additional resources.

The Challenge

Each client storefront ran on a different platform and catalog, and each served a distinct customer base within the UK market. The AI sales agent needed to be flexible enough to work across all of these stores without needing a full custom build for each one.

Opportunities

Because the agency serves multiple UK clients, a well-built AI sales agent could add value across the whole portfolio at once. It gave the agency a conversion capability they could roll out to every client they manage and to any new client they brought on.

Our Responsibilities

Designed an AI sales agent architecture built to support multiple storefronts from a single core

Configured the agent to adapt to each client's catalog and brand voice

Supported the agency team through rollout across all client accounts

Desired Outcomes

A repeatable AI sales agent setup the agency could deploy for any UK client

Higher conversion rates from visitors already arriving at client storefronts

Less manual intervention required from the agency team to improve per-client performance

A deployment model the agency could extend to new clients as their portfolio grew

The Vision

Cypherox designed the AI sales agent to be configured individually for each storefront without rebuilding the system each time. The agency team could adjust tone, product suggestions, and trigger conditions for each client brand through a configuration layer, not a new build.

The agent engages hesitant visitors in real time, adjusts its recommendations based on each client's catalog, and sends performance reports to the agency team on a per-account basis. This kept every client's data separate and gave the agency clear visibility across the portfolio.

Results and Business Impact

The agency can now offer a single repeatable AI Sales Agent deployment across all client storefronts, rather than scoping a custom build for each new UK account they bring on.

Hesitant traffic across the portfolio receives a consistent layer of real-time engagement, reducing the volume of manual conversion work that falls to the agency's internal team.
