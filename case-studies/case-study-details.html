The Problem

Scaling Payment Velocity Without
Compromising Security

As the client's retail and commercial user base tripled, their legacy rules-based fraud engine began creating critical operational bottlenecks across transactional settlement channels.

High Latency on Peak Days

During payday rushes and sales events, database write locks caused payment validation queues to backlog by up to 14 seconds, breaching internal core banking SLAs and triggering user retries.

Excessive False Positive Rate

Rigid legacy heuristics flagged over 6% of legitimate commercial transactions for manual human review, driving customer support escalations and slowing business payroll executions.

Strict FCA & PCI Compliance

Any replacement architecture required absolute end-to-end data auditability, deterministic explainability for flagged wires, and zero transmission of plaintext cardholder data outside isolated VPCs.

The Solution

An Event-Driven, Stream-First
ML Inference Pipeline

Cypherox embedded a dedicated squad of distributed systems engineers and ML practitioners to build a decoupled, zero-downtime transactional pipeline.

1. Distributed Kafka Message Bus

Replaced synchronous RPC calls with a distributed Apache Kafka cluster partitioned by customer hash, processing 14,000+ financial events per second with persistent replayability.

2. Sub-30ms ONNX Inference Runtime

Quantized multi-layer anomaly detection models compiled to ONNX runtime, evaluating 84 behavioral vectors (device fingerprint, geolocation delta, velocity) in just 14ms.

3. Active-Active Kubernetes (AWS EKS)

Microservices written in Go deployed across multi-region Kubernetes clusters with automated horizontal pod autoscaling and blue-green canary deployments.

4. Zero-Trust Security & Explainability

SHAP feature contribution values logged alongside every flagged decision into an immutable audit trail, ensuring 100% regulatory compliance with FCA reporting requirements.
