Our Story

Built from the Ground Up by Engineers

Cypherox was founded in 2015 by a small team of engineers who were frustrated by the gap between technology consulting and technology delivery. Too many agencies sold strategy but couldn't write production code. We set out to build something different — a company that leads with engineering depth.

Over the past 11 years, we've grown from a 6-person team in London to a globally distributed engineering organisation with specialists in AI, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, mobile, and data. Every engagement we take on is led by engineers, not project managers.

We serve some of the most demanding clients in financial services, healthcare, retail, and enterprise software — companies that cannot afford software that fails.

"We're not a typical agency. We are engineers who happen to run a business — and that changes everything about how we approach your project."

— James Davies, CEO & Co-Founder, Cypherox

Engineering Philosophy

How We Think About Building Software

Code Quality Is Non-Negotiable

We enforce automated testing, mandatory code review, and static analysis on every commit. Technical debt accumulates without discipline — we don't let it.

Security Is a Feature, Not a Layer

We integrate SAST, DAST, and dependency scanning into every CI pipeline. Security isn't added at the end — it's built into the first sprint.

Observability from Day One

Every production system we ship includes distributed tracing, structured logging, and alerting. You should never be surprised by an incident.

Documentation Is Part of Delivery

Architecture decision records, API docs, runbooks, and onboarding guides are deliverables — not afterthoughts.
