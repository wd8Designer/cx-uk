Stage Relevance

Startup IT Consulting Built Around Your Stage

The right guidance depends on where the business actually is, from pre-product idea validation through post-funding scale-up.

Pre-Seed and Idea Stage

Founders at this stage need clarity on what to build first and how to validate it without overcommitting resources.

  • Advises on minimum viable technical approach.
  • Helps prioritize features worth building versus testing manually.
  • Keeps early spend proportional to validation needs.

Seed Stage

Seed-stage startups need a foundation that supports early growth without slowing product iteration.

  • Reviews architecture against early growth expectations.
  • Balances speed of iteration with technical stability.
  • Identifies gaps before they affect product reliability.

Series A and Growth Stage

Growth-stage startups need to know whether current systems can support scaling users, team, and complexity.

  • Assesses systems against upcoming growth projections.
  • Prepares technical documentation for investor due diligence.
  • Plans hiring and architecture changes needed to scale.

Post Funding Scale Up

Post-funding startups need to move from founder-led technical decisions to a structured engineering function.

  • Supports transition to a formal engineering team structure.
  • Reviews technical debt accumulated during earlier stages.
  • Plans infrastructure investment aligned with funding runway.
