Legal and Professional Services

Legal translation requires precise handling of contracts and formal documents, where terminology errors carry real consequences.

  • Preserves formatting and clause structure in legal documents.
  • Applies consistent terminology across related contracts.
  • Flags ambiguous phrasing for human review before finalisation.

Ecommerce and Retail

Retail translation covers product listings, support content and customer communication across the markets a business sells into.

  • Translates product listings while keeping structured data intact.
  • Localises support content for tone and regional phrasing.
  • Scales with catalogue size across multiple languages.

Healthcare

Healthcare translation supports patient facing material and internal documentation, with accuracy treated as a priority over speed.

  • Maintains precise medical terminology across translated content.
  • Supports multilingual patient communication where required.
  • Routes sensitive or ambiguous content for human review.

Manufacturing and Technical Industries

Technical translation covers manuals, specifications and safety documentation where formatting and terminology consistency matter.

  • Preserves technical formatting across manuals and specifications.
